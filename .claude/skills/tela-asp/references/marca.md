# Marca: Radmila Bartuzi e o Método ASP

Distilled from the brand book (`Brand Book Radmila Bartuzi.html`, compiled 04/10/2026 from her brand
book, positioning documents, audios and site). Screen copy is pt-BR; this file quotes it as the screens
should say it.

## Who and what

- **Radmila Bartuzi**, health & wellness coach and sports nutritionist. Logo: a mountain-range symbol,
  "RADMILA BARTUZI" in thin geometric caps, "HEALTH & WELLNESS COACH" below (the logo asset is in the
  menu component; never redraw it).
- **Essence:** "Saúde começa na mente e se reflete no corpo." Performance serena.
- **Método ASP**, three phases in order: **Autoconsciência**, **Saúde**, **Performance**. The screens
  already show the participant's current phase ("Você está na fase Saúde").
- **Programs** (the product ladder, first step to deepest): Ritual de Prioridade (5 days, digital, entry
  point) · Inicie sua Saúde (group mentoring, 12 weekly encontros) · Cuide da sua Saúde (group, 6
  encontros) · Transforme sua Saúde (group, performance and shape without pressure) · Performance Serena
  (individual premium, WhatsApp support).
- **How she works:** weekly encontros where the week's goals are agreed, structured feedback (daily on
  premium, weekly on basic), food-diary feedback, gamification, celebrating real milestones.

## Who uses the platform

- **Participante:** adults, mostly women 25 to 50, active professional life, exhausted by hustle culture
  and rigid rules ("A Buscadora Cansada de Regras"), and amateur athletes. She gets things done once
  there is a routine; empty open days make her procrastinate; she loses herself in her own organisation.
  She wants autonomy: to learn to observe herself, not a drawer prescription.
- **Admin (Radmila and staff):** follows many participants; needs to see who needs attention.

What they value: emotional safety (never judged), **critério** ("isso serve / isso não serve"),
acolhimento followed by direction, coherence, discretion and maturity. Authority is solidity, not
hardness.

## Voice in the interface

Sober, warm, adult, second person, short. Quiet luxury: sophistication without show. The dictionary
has "Bora!" and "Bem a sua cara!" but the positioning moved to sobriety: use them at most in a
celebration, never in chrome.

Word swaps the positioning asks for:

| Instead of                                   | Say                                      |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| resultado                                    | travessia, caminho                       |
| foco, disciplina                             | presença, constância, critério           |
| prova (as proof)                             | experiência vivida                       |
| falhou, atrasada, perdeu                     | not marked, open, "ainda não"            |
| comunidade, tribo, família fitness, time     | the person's own space, the turma        |
| dieta, cardápio, macros, calorias, restrição | alimentação, escolhas, rotina, estrutura |

The interface never scolds. A missed day is an empty square, never red, never a warning. A done thing
is pressed in terracota and stays modest ("Feita").

Phrases that are hers and can close an empty state or a note: "Seu corpo merece estratégia, não
pressão." · "Consistência de dentro pra fora." · "Não existe milagre, existe presença." · "Pequenas
decisões que movem montanhas." · "Começar do simples."

## Her world, for content

- Rituals: shot matinal and water in the morning, chá or a healthy drink in the afternoon, meditation
  and yoga, conscious pauses, weekly summary and planning, encontro on Monday to set the week's goals,
  "sexta é dia de preto no treino".
- What gets celebrated: races completed, podiums, workouts done, food diary kept, habit and behaviour
  changes, handling an emotionally hard situation differently.
- Areas the goals fall in (as the screens label them): Hidratação, Movimento, Alimentação, Sono, Mente,
  Rotina.
- Imagery (if a screen ever needs a picture): natural strong light, raw wood, burnt concrete, natural
  fabric, real skin and sweat, water in movement, mountains. Inspiration: Alo Wellness, Nike Women slow
  motion. The platform screens so far use no photographs.

A number a screen needs and this list does not give is drawn as sample data of the same participant and

## Pontos em aberto

The brand sources disagree on these; a screen that touches one says which side it took:

- Method name: "Método ASP" (brand book) vs "Abordagem ASP" / "Lente ASP" (site, pitch). The platform
  says Método ASP.
- Which performance: podium (brand book) vs serena/leve (positioning). The platform leans serena.
- Community: the brand book wants tribe names and the Conscious Club; the positioning calls "comunidade,
  tribo, família fitness" a fatal error.
- Nutrition with or without cardápio: the positioning says she does not work with dieta, cardápio or
  macros.
